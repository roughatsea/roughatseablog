Complexity sends bills that often arrive silently.

When I add something, I ask: what exact benefit does this buy that I can't get another way?

If the answer is unclear, treat the change as reversible. Prefer a small experiment over a large irreversible layer.

Short checklist:

- Who benefits today?
- What will be harder to explain in six months?
- Is there a smaller surface that achieves the same effect?

Keep the answers as part of the note—context helps the future reader decide whether the layer still pays its rent.
