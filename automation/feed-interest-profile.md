# Feed interest profile

Last revised: 2026-10-24. Scope: editorial selection for the public Rough at Sea feed.

Read this file in full on every run alongside `automation/daily-feed.md`. This profile records the interests discussed with the owner; it is not a complete biography, a psychological assessment, or evidence for factual claims. Do not depend on conversation memory being available. These are starting points for discovery, not a checklist for each edition.

## What attracts the reader

- **Software engineering with substance:** How languages and systems work; unusual language behavior, pointers, algorithms, architecture, tooling, and explanations that make difficult concepts clear. Explain concrete code before compressed terminology.
- **Making games:** Emotionally resonant JRPGs, discovery-driven narrative and exploration, believable worlds, expressive procedural animation, and systems that respect the player's time. Relevant design questions include alternatives to grinding and random encounters.
- **Games and anime with artistic identity:** Evangelion, Xenogears, Chrono Cross, Final Fantasy, and NieR are taste references for atmosphere, mystery, philosophical ambition, strong music, and emotional force. Seek original works with comparable ambition, rather than requiring superficial resemblance.
- **Specific projects to follow:** Final Fantasy XIV: Evercold; its announced Evangelion crossover raid series, Evangelion – Ghosts of Desire; GROTESQQQUE; and the announced Evangelion anime project involving Yoko Taro and Keiichi Okabe. Verify identities, current official announcements, production roles, and release details each time. Follow substantive changes and legal U.S. availability, including Tucson/Phoenix screenings where relevant; avoid spoilers and recycled announcements. These watch targets do not imply daily coverage or authorization to change the separate watchlist task.
- **Extraordinary music:** Distinctive composition, emotional texture, and artistic imagination. Reference points include “Costumed” by Christopher Emerle (also known as Irrelevant), Radiohead, The Strokes, and Warpaint. Obscurity is not itself merit. Do not invent listening impressions when audio was not accessible; verify direct listening links.
- **Writing and literary craft:** Voice, humor, characterization, discovery writing, emotionally powerful fiction, and practical ways to move from possibilities to actual stories. Tools matter when they support the writer's agency and exploration.
- **Literature in context:** Who came first, publication chronology, plausible and documented influence, and what an important work contributes. Temporal overlap alone does not establish influence.
- **Seminal and neglected ideas:** Philosophy, psychology, political thought, economics, intellectual history, and unfamiliar ideas that can change a reader's conceptual vocabulary. Include non-Western traditions and serious alternatives; distinguish historical importance from empirical validity.
- **Existence and identity:** Consciousness, personal identity, death, possible resurrection, continuity of a particular individual, and the physical limits of what reality might permit. Clearly separate philosophical argument, established science, and speculation.
- **Creativity and inspiration:** Sudden or apparently foreign ideas, historical accounts of inspiration, and scientific explanations. Preserve the interesting question without implying that a paranormal explanation has been established.
- **Space and grounded speculation:** Interstellar settlement, generation ships, planetary habitability, propulsion, life support, and explicit engineering tradeoffs. Separate calculations, assumptions, and speculative technology from demonstrated capability.
- **Worldbuilding under radically different conditions:** Post-scarcity societies, abundance, reinvention, freedom from imposed ideology, extreme experiences, and what people pursue when survival is no longer the organizing constraint.
- **Biology and medicine:** Laboratory science, blood cells, immunology, metabolism, and mechanisms connected to observable outcomes. Prefer sound evidence and meaningful advances over wellness hype; distinguish laboratory findings from demonstrated human benefit.
- **Mathematics made understandable:** Worked examples, history, applications, and interactive demonstrations that develop intuition, including accessible windows into advanced subjects.
- **Visual and interactive understanding:** Simulations, traversable 3D models, algorithm visualizations, deterministic graphics, and tools that make abstract ideas explorable. Favor actual working examples over presentation alone.
- **Economic security and independence:** Compensation, labor conditions, household purchasing power, entrepreneurship, SaaS, royalties, and realistic paths toward control over one's time. Explain costs, constraints, incentives, and who benefits; avoid get-rich-quick claims.
- **Politics through human consequences:** Whether institutions enable decent lives, how power and incentives operate, and differences between shareholder, employee, and public outcomes. Consider human welfare and distribution, while testing claims fairly and including credible evidence that challenges expectations. Do not select facts merely to affirm an assumed political identity.
- **Parenthood, learning, and accessibility:** Child development, language acquisition, books, literacy, inclusive design, accessible recreation, and useful ideas that enrich family life. Keep coverage general and public-facing; do not infer or disclose personal medical circumstances.
- **Tucson and Arizona:** Local culture, food, worthwhile events, infrastructure, public decisions, environmental conditions, and changes that materially affect communities. Include clear dates and practical details when verified.
- **Mexican language and culture:** Everyday Spanish, idioms, humor, music, history, and the cultural context behind them; avoid stereotypes and treating one regional tradition as universal.
- **Beauty, coziness, and existential tenderness:** Warmth, wonder, affection for living things, emotionally resonant art, and the bittersweet awareness that life changes. Joy and beauty can earn a place without a productivity justification.
- **Vivid visual imagination:** Saturated color, striking artistic direction, unusual architecture, and playful or awe-inspiring environments. Include visual work when there is something substantive to see or explore.

## Selection principles

Use the interests to explain why a particular development merits attention, rather than merely matching a topic word. Favor depth, unusual connections, intellectual or emotional significance, demonstrated usefulness, and important consequences. For example, a well-supported advance in habitat life support may be more relevant than another routine launch headline; an exceptional musical discovery may outrank a celebrity news cycle.

Balance familiarity with expansion. Give unfamiliar fields, places, artists, and perspectives a fair chance. A discovery must offer more than novelty or obscurity. No daily quota for individual subjects; rotate over time and avoid repeatedly filling the edition with AI and games.

Cover consequential events even without an interest match. Public health, civil rights, conflict, institutions, economic conditions, and environmental risks can deserve attention because of their effects on other people. Present the concrete development and context, avoiding performative outrage, false balance, and unsupported certainty.

Known interests are not proof that a candidate is worthwhile, and this profile does not establish current facts. Verify every claim and specific watch target with current sources. Clearly label older discoveries and inference. Do not manufacture a personal connection or use the owner's private life in public summaries.

## Keeping the profile useful

Only revise durable preferences from explicit user instructions or clear user feedback. Do not treat silence, one selected story, or one speculative inference as a new permanent interest. Preserve variety without silently narrowing the profile. Incorporate future corrections and additions here so subsequent runs receive them. The scheduled publishing task does not independently authorize broad profile changes.
